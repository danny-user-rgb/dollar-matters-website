Bring Dollar Matters to Your School

Free financial literacy workshops for high schools and community centers. We come to you.

What to expect

Our workshops are designed for high school students and adult learners in community settings. Trained college volunteers lead engaging, interactive sessions that make money concepts real and accessible — no lectures, no textbooks.

  • Sessions run 45–90 minutes, customizable to your schedule
  • We bring all materials — slides, handouts, and activities
  • Works in classrooms, libraries, gyms, or community halls
  • Available in English and Spanish
  • Completely free, always

Topics we can cover

Saving Budgeting Credit Scores Compound Interest Predatory Lending Filing Taxes

We can tailor a single session or a multi-session series. Tell us what your students need most and we'll build around it.
